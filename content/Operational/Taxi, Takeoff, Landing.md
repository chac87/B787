## Crosswind Takeoff

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Aileron</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>INCREASING INTO THE WIND</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Start with the control wheel centered, then apply increasing aileron into the wind to keep the wings level</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Until 80 kts</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>SLIGHT FORWARD PRESSURE</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Nose slightly pushed down – prevents early nose wheel lift-off</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>80–100 kts</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>NEUTRAL</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Release forward pressure, normal roll attitude</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>v<sub>R</sub> – Rotation</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>WINGS LEVEL</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Rotation to OEI pitch + 1,5° in 5 sec</div>

</div>

> [!info] Crosswind Technique
> The aircraft weathervanes into the wind automatically at lift-off (drift). No crabbing on the runway required – establish the crab only after lift-off.

## Gear Up & Flap Retraction

**Positive rate** is confirmed on the **altimeter** – not on the VSI (unreliable right after lift-off).

Flap retraction in VNAV: automatically at the altitude from TAKEOFF REF 2/2 in the FMC.

> [!info] Flap Retraction Logic
> T/O flap > 5: **first call is always Flap 5** – no intermediate settings.
> Then: call for the next flap setting when passing each speed bug.
> Briefly dropping slightly below flap maneuver speed is acceptable on the 787.

**Flaps UP bug** = v<sub>REF</sub> 30 + 80 kts → margin of at least 40° bank angle to stick shaker.

## Crosswind Landing

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>latest 500 ft</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MANUAL FLIGHT</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>30 ft</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>FLARE</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>10 ft</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>DECRAB WITH RUDDER</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">If needed, aileron to keep wings level</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>After Landing</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAINTAIN AILERON – GRADUALLY RELEASE</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Keep the centerline with rudder</div>

</div>
