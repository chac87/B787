# 8.8 Oxygen Requirements

## 8.8 - Cockpit Oxygen

Use Cockpit oxygen if:

- Cabin alt >10000 ft for more than 30 min, but <=13000 ft
- Cabin altitude >13000 ft
- Only 1 pilot at station >FL410 / 12500 m (>FL430 / 13100 m mit Auto Emer Descent function)
