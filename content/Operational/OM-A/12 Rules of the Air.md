# 12 Rules of the Air

## 12.1 - Minimum levels within 8 km

- **High terrain:** 2000 ft AGL
- **Outside high terrain:** 1000 ft AGL

## 12.3 - Com failure

(ref. OM-C >Com 2.2 and >Europe 5.1)

- Squawk 7600
- Thereafter maintain level and speed for 7 min (20 min within Europe oder in airspaces ohne ATS surveillance)
- Continue according OFP to an appropriate Navaid or Fix at destination
- Start descend at or close to the ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) or EAT (Expected Approach Time) last given
- Follow instrument approach and land within 30 min after ETA/EAT

## 12.4.2 - Interception signals

**Interceptor to aircraft:**

- **"You have been intercepted, follow me":**
  - Rocking wings & nav lights flashing irregularly
  - Wenn verstanden, rock wings & flash nav lights irregularly
- **"You may proceed":**
  - Abrupt climbing breakaway of interceptor
  - Wenn verstanden, rock wings
- **"Land here":**
  - Lowered gear, illuminated landing light & overflying runway
  - Wenn verstanden, check runway to be safe, thereafter lower gear & turn landing lights on

**Aircraft to interceptor:**

- **"Runway inadequate":**
  - Raise gear and flash landing lights while overhead the runway
  - Wenn verstanden by interceptor, he raises gear, thereafter rock wings & flash nav lights irregularly ("understood, follow me")
- **"Cannot comply":**
  - Flash all lights in a regular manner
  - Wenn verstanden by interceptor, abrupt breakaway by him
- **"MAYDAY":**
  - Flash all lights irregularly
  - Wenn verstanden by interceptor, abrupt breakaway by him

## 12.5 - Headsets

Use headsets:

- From Eng start until reaching 1st cruising level
- From TOD till parking position
- One CM leaves cockpit (TCAS RA)
- Receiving ATC-clearance via voice

## 12.6.1.1.1 - Airport light & pyrotechnic signals

- **Steady green:** cleared to land
- **Steady red:** give way, continue circling
- **Flashing green:** return for landing
- **Flashing red:** airport unsafe, do not land
- **Flashing white:** land here
- **Red fireworks:** do not land, standby

**Aircraft answers "Yes":** rock wings or flash landing lights twice

## 12.8.3.1 - Meldung von Abweichungen

- **ETO:** >=3 min (nicht notwendig bei einer aktiven ADS-C-connection)
- **Speed:** >=+/-0.02 M; >=+/-10 kts TAS
