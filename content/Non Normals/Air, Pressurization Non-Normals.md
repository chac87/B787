# Air, Pressurization Non-Normals

> [!info] A/C Reset Switch
> The A/C Reset Switch resets the following components:
> - Pack valve
> - Trim air valve
> - Recirculation fan
> - Fault protection

## <span class="c-red">CABIN ALTITUDE</span>

- See also [[Memory Items]]
- When cabin pressure is lost, the outflow valves close automatically to preserve cabin pressure. During the emergency descent they remain closed until the airplane reaches 15.000 ft or descends below the current cabin altitude. They then open fully for the remainder of the flight.
- Before deploying passenger oxygen: verify it is actually necessary (they drop automatically if cabin altitude exceeds 14.500 ft)

## <span class="c-amber">STBY COOLING</span>

This advisory message shows if the pack is in the standby cooling mode. STBY COOLING shows on the synoptic. At low altitudes and high outside air temperatures it is possible that the pack will shut down.

## <span class="c-amber">PACK L+R</span>

The airplane will not stay pressurized if the 2 packs are off. You must descend to a safe altitude, or 10.000 feet. After 2 minutes, you can try to reset the packs — see <a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/PACK-L+R"><span>PACK L+R</span></a>.

If the system will not reset, continue to descend and level off at a safe altitude. When the airplane has depressurized, push the ventilation switch (AVS).

> [!info] Alternate Ventilation System
> The alternate ventilation system positions the FWD outflow valve in the ten o'clock position and the aft outflow valve in the one o'clock position. It supplies fresh outside air to the flight deck and cabin through the air distribution manifold.
