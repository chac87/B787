# Altimeter Error

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The current altitude indication on a PFD is incorrect. (Reasons to suspect a current altitude indication error are listed in the Additional Information section.)<br>Objective: To stop an airplane altitude oscillation if it is occurring, to identify a reliable current altitude indication, and to restore autothrottle operation if the autothrottles disconnected.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">1</span><strong>Altitude HOLD switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Verify the MCP selected altitude is the desired altitude.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>FLCH switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>An airplane pitch and altitude oscillation can continue while the autopilot reaches the correct altitude.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>When</strong> airplane altitude is level:</div>
<div class="nnc-step">&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The <strong>captain's</strong> current altitude indication is the same as the MCP selected altitude:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 6</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The <strong>first officer's</strong> current altitude indication is the same as the MCP selected altitude:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 8</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>Tuning and control panel TRANSPONDER XPDR SYS</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>L</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Use the captain's current altitude indication for the rest of the flight.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Use the A/P engage switch on the captain's side of the MCP for the rest of the flight.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">7&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The autothrottles are <strong>disconnected:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Disconnect the autopilot.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>A/P engage switch on the captain's side of the MCP</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Autothrottle is available.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 10</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The autothrottles are <strong>not</strong> disconnected:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 10</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">8</span><strong>Tuning and control panel TRANSPONDER XPDR SYS</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>R</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Use the first officer's current altitude indication for the rest of the flight.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Use the A/P engage switch on the first officer's side of the MCP for the rest of the flight.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">9&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The autothrottles are <strong>disconnected:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Disconnect the autopilot.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>A/P engage switch on the first officer's side of the MCP</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Autothrottle is available.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 10</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The autothrottles are <strong>not</strong> disconnected:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 10</div>

<div class="nnc-step">10&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The difference between captain's and first officer's current altitude indications is <strong>greater than 200 feet:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Altimeter error is reduced below 20,000 feet.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not use VNAV at or above 20,000 feet. Use ALT HOLD and FLCH as needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">The difference between captain's and first officer's current altitude indications is <strong>200 feet or less:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">VNAV is available.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> V/S and FPA are unreliable.</div>

<div class="nnc-separator"></div>

<div class="nnc-box"><strong>Additional Information</strong><br><br>A current altitude indication error can be caused by a fault in the air data system. This affects each PFD separately.<br><br>Reasons to suspect an altitude indication error due to a fault in the air data system:<br>• The captain's and first officer's current altitude indications are different<br>• One current altitude indication is different from the MCP selected altitude<br>• The autopilot commands the airplane to climb or descend without pilot input<br>• The airplane pitch and altitude oscillates without pilot input<br>• The autothrottles disconnect without pilot input</div>
