<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If using GPS altitude, the altimeter cannot be used to accurately maintain ATC-assigned altitudes.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Transponder altitude and TCAS indications may be erroneous. Consider selecting ALT RPTG OFF on the TRANSPONDER MODE selector.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Run the engine and wing anti-ice systems manually.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Thrust asymmetry protection is inoperative. Maintain airspeed at or above 130 knots.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span>Avoid abrupt control inputs and high maneuvering loads. Airplane response is changed by simplified control laws.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">10</span><strong>Thrust levers (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Retard to mid position</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">This prevents exceeding the thrust limits when switching to the alternate EEC mode.</div>

<div class="nnc-caution">⚠&ensp;Push one switch at a time.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">11</span><strong>EEC MODE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Maximum thrust limiting is not available with autothrottle disconnected. Alternate thrust setting information shows on the N1 indications.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">12</span><strong>ANTI-ICE selectors (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON or OFF as needed</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-sub">Envelope protection functions inop</div>

<div class="nnc-box"><strong>A ground proximity warning system fault occurs:</strong><br>Some or all ground proximity alerts are not available. Ground proximity alerts that occur are valid.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not use the autopilot, autothrottle, or flight directors.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">The yaw damper is degraded.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Manual control inputs are needed to compensate for asymmetric thrust conditions.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Plan to reduce gross weight to less than Maximum Landing Weight before extending flaps. Maneuver margin can be limited while flaps are extending from UP to 5.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Plan more time for alternate flap and slat extension. Allow 3 minutes to extend flaps to 20.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Use flaps 20 and v<sub>REF</sub> 20 for landing. If v<sub>REF</sub> 20 is less than 130 knots, use 130 knots for v<sub>REF</sub> 20. This ensures sufficient pitch trim and directional control capability for landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not use TO/GA for a go-around or missed approach.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Avoid speedbrake use in flight.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not arm the speedbrake lever. This prevents inadvertent in-flight speedbrake extension.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Manually extend the speedbrakes after landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Check the Non-Normal Configuration Landing Distance tables in the Performance Inflight-QRH chapter or other approved source.</div>
