# Bomb On Board

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: A specific threat that a bomb is on board, or a suspected or confirmed bomb is on board.<br>Objective:<br>• to reduce cabin differential pressure<br>• to relocate Bomb to LRBL if remaining flight time is greater than 30 minutes</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Level off as required. When climbing, initiate level off if obstacle clearance is assured.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>LDG ALT selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>PULL ON</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">MAN landing altitude mode shows on EICAS.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>LDG ALT selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set to present CAB ALTITUDE</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>Cabin</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Inform</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>Passenger Signs</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>IFE / FLYNET</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Inform Purser to turn OFF</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>ATC / Company</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Notify</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-caution"><strong>Warning:</strong> Avoid abrupt maneuvers.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">8</span></div>
<div class="nnc-step">&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing <strong>possible</strong> within 30 minutes:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 18</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing <strong>not</strong> possible within 30 minutes:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 9</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span>Consider low IAS.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">10</span>Descent to CAB ALT +2.500 ft or MEA / MORA if higher.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Maintaining a flight altitude of cabin altitude +2.500 ft will lead to a differential pressure of 1 psi.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">11</span><strong>When</strong> at CAB ALT +2.500 ft:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Instruct cabin crew to move suspect device to LRBL if possible (Door 4R)</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">12</span>Maintain 1 psi differential pressure.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">13</span><strong>Fuel Reserves</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Determine</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">14</span></div>
<div class="nnc-step">&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Fuel reserves <strong>sufficient</strong> to continue in landing configuration:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 15</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Fuel reserves <strong>not</strong> sufficient to continue in landing configuration:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 17</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">15</span><strong>Flaps</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Extend</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">16</span><strong>Landing Gear lever (except over water)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Down</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">This ensures that the aircraft is configured before damage to aircraft systems after a bomb explosion prevent further extension of flaps and landing gear.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not use FMC performance predictions with the gear and flaps extended. Refer to the OM-B chapter 4.7.2 Landing Gear Extended for flight planning.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">17</span>During further descent maintain 1 psi differential pressure.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">18</span><strong>Disembarkation / Evacuation</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Prepare</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">19</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• CABIN ALTITUDE AUTO</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• LANDING ALTITUDE</div>
