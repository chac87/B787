# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> GPS

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Both GPS receivers are failed.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> GPS position data is unavailable. Navigation capability may be reduced. If the equipment and navigation aids for all other approach types are inoperative, instrument approach capability may be unavailable.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> ADS-B is inoperative.</div>
