<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The flap/slat electronics units are failed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Set airspeed at or below flap limit speed for selected flap position.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>If</strong> flap retraction is needed:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>FLAP lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>UP</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>Alternate flaps selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>RET</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Monitor airspeed during retraction.</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>Tuning and control panel GPWS FLAP OVRD</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OVRD</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Plan to reduce gross weight to less than Maximum Landing Weight before extending flaps. Maneuver margin can be limited while flaps are extending from UP to 5.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Plan more time for alternate flap and slat extension. Allow 3 minutes to extend flaps to 20.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Use flaps 20 and v<sub>REF</sub> 20 for landing. The alternate mode is limited to a maximum of flaps 20.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Check the Non-Normal Configuration Landing Distance tables in the Performance Inflight-QRH chapter or other approved source.</div>
