# Smoke, Fire or Fumes

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Smoke, fire, or fumes occur.<br>Objective: To remove power from the ignition source. To land the airplane as soon as possible, if needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Diversion may be needed.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Don the oxygen masks and smoke goggles, if needed.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Establish crew and cabin communications.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>IFE/PASS SEATS power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>RECIRC FANS switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">6</span>Any time the smoke or fumes become the greatest threat:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;<a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/Smoke-or-Fumes-Removal">Go to the Smoke or Fumes Removal checklist</a></div>

<div class="nnc-step">7&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Source of the smoke, fire, or fumes is <strong>obvious and can</strong> be extinguished quickly:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Isolate and extinguish the source of the smoke, fire, or fumes.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">If practical, remove power from the affected equipment by switch or circuit breaker in the flight deck or cabin.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 8</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Source of the smoke, fire, or fumes is <strong>not obvious or cannot</strong> be extinguished quickly:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 9</div>

<div class="nnc-step">8&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Source is visually confirmed to be extinguished and smoke or fumes are <strong>decreasing:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Continue the flight at the captain's discretion.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Restore unpowered items at the captain's discretion.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;<a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/Smoke-or-Fumes-Removal">Go to the Smoke or Fumes Removal checklist, if needed</a></div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Smoke or fumes <strong>continue:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 9</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span>Advise the cabin that the main cabin lighting will be turned off.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">10</span><strong>CABIN/UTILITY power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">11</span>Start a diversion to the nearest suitable airport while continuing the checklist.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">12</span>Consider an immediate landing if the smoke, fire, or fumes situation becomes uncontrollable.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">13</span>Do not delay landing in an attempt to complete the following steps.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">14</span><strong>L PACK switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">15</span><strong>L TRIM AIR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">16</span>Wait 2 minutes unless the smoke or fumes are increasing. This allows time for the smoke or fumes to clear.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">17</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• PACK L</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• TRIM AIR L</div>

<div class="nnc-step">18&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Smoke or fumes <strong>continue:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="cl-item"><strong>L PACK switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>AUTO</strong></div>
  <div class="cl-item"><strong>L TRIM AIR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>
  <div class="cl-item"><strong>R PACK switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
  <div class="cl-item"><strong>R TRIM AIR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 19</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Smoke or fumes are <strong>decreasing:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;<a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/Smoke-or-Fumes-Removal">Go to the Smoke or Fumes Removal checklist, if needed</a></div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">19</span>Wait 2 minutes unless the smoke or fumes are increasing. This allows time for the smoke or fumes to clear.</div>

<div class="nnc-sub">• PACK R</div>

</div>
