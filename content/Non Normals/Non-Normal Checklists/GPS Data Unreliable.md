# GPS Data Unreliable

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Erroneous or invalid GPS data is suspected.</div>

<div class="nnc-caution">GPS interference can alter the airplane flight path without obvious flight deck indications or effects.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>If at any time, the GPS message shows:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to the GPS checklist</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-step">2 &nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">On the <strong>ground</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">Use Supplementary Procedures or operator-originated standard operating procedures for intentional operations in areas of known GPS interference.</div>
</div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">In <strong>flight</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 3</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>In flight, one or more of the following can indicate unreliable GPS data:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">PULL UP alert that occurs with sufficient, immediately verifiable terrain clearance</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Slow increase in ANP</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Change in terrain data on the ND and VSD that does not match aeronautical charts</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">VSD TERR flag on the VSD</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">FMC position on the ND changes to DME-DME or INERTIAL</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Inaccurate UTC TIME/DATE and FMC-generated ETA</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">HUD flight path vector misalignment</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Excessive lateral deviation when intercepting the localizer or final approach course</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Unable to log on to ATC datalink with inaccurate UTC TIME/DATE</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>The following alerts can occur and must be considered valid:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">ADS-B OUT</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">FMC VERIFY POSITION</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">GND PROX SYS</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">NAV UNABLE RNP</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">TERR POS</div>
</div>
