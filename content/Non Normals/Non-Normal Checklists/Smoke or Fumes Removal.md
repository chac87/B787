# Smoke or Fumes Removal

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

> [!info] This NNC can be applied at any altitude!

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Smoke or fumes removal is needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Do this checklist <strong>only</strong> when directed by the <a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/Smoke,-Fire-or-Fumes">Smoke, Fire or Fumes checklist</a>.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Do not delay landing in an attempt to complete the following steps.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Close the flight deck door.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">4&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Most smoke or fumes are in the cabin <strong>forward</strong> of mid-wing:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 5</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Most smoke or fumes are in the cabin <strong>aft</strong> of mid-wing:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 9</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>EQUIP COOLING FWD switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> After 30 minutes of operation at low altitude and low cabin differential pressure, non-critical electronic equipment may fail.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>AFT OUTFLOW VALVE switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>AFT OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Hold in CLOSE until the outflow valve indication shows fully closed</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">8</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• EQUIP CLG OVRD FWD</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• OUTFLOW VALVE AFT</div>
