# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> PACK L, R

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The affected pack is inoperative.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> With only one pack operating, cabin altitude can climb above normal, up to 8.000 ft, when the airplane is near maximum operating altitude. Pressurization is normal at lower cruise altitudes.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Do not attempt an air conditioning reset above 34.000 ft.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If the PACK L+R message shows at any time, do the <a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/PACK-L+R"><span>PACK L+R checklist</span></a>.</div>

<div class="nnc-cl">

</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude <strong>is at or below</strong> 34.000 ft:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 6</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-step">3 &ensp;&ensp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Descent to 34.000 ft or lower is <strong>practical</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 4</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Descent to 34.000 ft or lower is <strong>not practical</strong>:</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>Start a descent to 34.000 ft or lower.</div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Attempt only one reset for this condition per flight.</div>
