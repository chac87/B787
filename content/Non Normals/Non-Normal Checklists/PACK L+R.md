# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> PACK L+R

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Both packs are inoperative.<br>Objective: To prevent excessive cabin altitude and temperature by descending and increasing ventilation.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Don the oxygen masks.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Establish crew communications.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>Without delay</strong>, start a descent to the lowest safe altitude or 10.000 ft, whichever is higher.</div>

<div class="nnc-box">
  <strong>To descend:</strong>
  <div class="nnc-sub">Move the thrust levers to idle</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">Extend the speedbrakes</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">If structural integrity is in doubt, limit airspeed and avoid high maneuvering loads</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">Descend at v<sub>MO</sub>/M<sub>MO</sub></div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>Plan to land at the nearest suitable airport.</div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Do not attempt an air conditioning reset above 34.000 ft.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>⚠&ensp;AIR COND RESET switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push and hold for 1 second</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>Wait</strong> 2 minutes unless the PACK L+R message shows again.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">7 &ensp;&ensp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">PACK L+R message <strong>blanks</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">PACK L+R message <strong>shows</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 8</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">8</span><strong>IFE/PASS SEATS power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> The IFE/passenger seats switch can be turned ON again when needed (such as moving powered seats for landing). Select the IFE/passenger seats switch off again when no longer needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">9</span><strong>CABIN/UTILITY power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">10</span><strong>SHOULDER and FOOT HEATERS controls (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>LOW</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">11</span>Minimize flight deck lighting intensity.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">12</span>During daylight, install the flight deck sun visors.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">13</span><strong>When</strong> at level off:</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not exceed 260 knots when the airplane is below 6.000 ft.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">14</span><strong>VENTILATION switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ALTN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">15</span>Selecting the VENTILATION switch to ALTN automatically reconfigures both outflow valves to optimal flow positions and can take up to 3 minutes to reduce cabin differential pressure.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">16</span><strong>Wait</strong> 3 minutes.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">17</span>The correct outflow valve positions are:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Forward outflow valve approximately 10 o'clock position</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Aft outflow valve 1 o'clock position</div>

<div class="nnc-step">18 &ensp;&ensp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Both outflow valve positions are <strong>correct</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the alternate ventilation switch to NORM or ALTN as needed.</div>
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 23</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">One or both outflow valve positions are <strong>incorrect</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 19</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">19</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">20</span><strong>⚠&ensp;FWD OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move the forward outflow valve until the indication shows at the 10 o'clock position</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">21</span><strong>⚠&ensp;AFT OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move to OPEN until the outflow valve indication shows at the 1 o'clock position</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the aft outflow valve between fully closed and the 1 o'clock position as needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">22</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklist:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">CABIN ALTITUDE AUTO</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">23</span>If needed to reduce flight deck temperature, open the flight deck door.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">24</span>If needed to reduce flight deck temperature, open the flight deck overhead vent. Open the overhead door cover in the ceiling behind the first officer's seat. Then open the overhead vent cover panel for access to the flight deck overhead vent.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Opening the overhead vent results in a very loud flight deck that can cause difficulty in communications. If unable to maintain communications due to noise, close the vent.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold, the crew can:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Push the cabin/utility power switch ON</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Push the IFE/passenger seats power switch ON</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Select the shoulder and foot heaters controls above LOW</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Close the flight deck overhead vent</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Open or close the flight deck door</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Bulk cargo heat is inoperative.</div>
