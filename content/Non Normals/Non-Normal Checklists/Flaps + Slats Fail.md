# Flaps + Slats Fail

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: FLAPS DRIVE and SLATS DRIVE messages have shown during the flight.<br>Objective: To determine the correct flap setting and v<sub>REF</sub>.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Do this checklist <strong>only</strong> when directed by either the FLAPS DRIVE or the SLATS DRIVE checklist.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Do not use alternate flaps. Asymmetry and uncommanded motion protection is not provided in the alternate mode.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>Tuning and control panel GPWS FLAP OVRD</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OVRD</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not use FMC performance predictions with flaps or slats extended.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Consider reducing gross weight to lower the approach speed.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not use autoland.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Maintain airspeed at or above the flaps up maneuver speed until established on final approach.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Use current flaps and v<sub>REF</sub> 30 + 55 or top of amber band, whichever is slower. Do not use any additional additives.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Plan to operate the thrust levers manually during approach, landing, and missed approach.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span></div>
<div class="nnc-step">&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing airport elevation is <strong>greater than</strong> 2.500 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Refer to the Tire Speed Landing Limit Weight table in the Performance Inflight chapter. If landing weight exceeds the limit, reduce gross weight or divert to a lower elevation airport.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 5</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing airport elevation is <strong>less than or equal to</strong> 2.500 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 5</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• FLAPS DRIVE</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• FLAPS PRIMARY FAIL</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• SLATS DRIVE</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• SLATS PRIMARY FAIL</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Check the Non-Normal Configuration Landing Distance tables in the Performance Inflight-QRH chapter or other approved source.</div>
