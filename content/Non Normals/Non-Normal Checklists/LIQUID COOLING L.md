# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> LIQUID COOLING L

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: A left liquid cooling system fault occurs.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Cooling for the large motor power system may not be available. The following items may be inoperative:<br>• L pack<br>• L center fuel pump<br>• C1 electric pump<br>• R demand hydraulic pump</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Equipment may become operative or inoperative later in the flight.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">If both the LIQUID COOLING L and LIQUID COOLING R messages show at any time, start a descent to the lowest safe altitude or 10.000 ft, whichever is higher. When the PACK L+R message shows, do the PACK L+R checklist.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not exceed 260 knots when the airplane is below 6.000 ft.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Right thrust reverser deployment may be slower than normal.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• PACK L</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• HYD PRESS C1</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• HYD PRESS DEM R</div>
