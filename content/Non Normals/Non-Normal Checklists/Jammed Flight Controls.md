# Jammed Flight Controls

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Flight controls are jammed or restricted in roll, pitch, or yaw.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">1</span><strong>Autopilot disconnect switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>Autothrottle disconnect switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Overpower the jammed or restricted system.</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">Use maximum force, including a combined effort of both pilots, as needed. A maximum two-pilot effort on the controls will not damage the flight controls.</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-step">4 &nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Controls are <strong>normal</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 5</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Controls stay <strong>jammed or restricted</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-note">Control may be limited to one pilot.<br><br>If the aileron controls are jammed, use rudder and rudder trim to counter the effect of the jam.<br><br>If the elevator controls are jammed, use pitch trim to counter the effect of the jam. If more pitch trim authority is needed, select DISC on the primary flight computers disconnect switch. This puts the flight control system in direct mode. In direct mode, pitch trim moves slower than in normal mode.<br><br>If the rudder controls are jammed, use aileron or engage the autopilot to counter the effect of the jam.<br><br>Do not make abrupt thrust changes.<br><br>Extend or retract speedbrake slowly and smoothly.<br><br>Limit bank angle to 15 degrees.<br><br>The autopilot and autothrottle may be available to control the airplane.<br><br>Do not use autoland.<br><br>If the rudder controls are jammed, differential braking may be needed for landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 5</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span>Plan to land at the nearest suitable airport.</div>
