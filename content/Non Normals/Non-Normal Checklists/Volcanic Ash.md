# Volcanic Ash

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Volcanic ash is suspected when one or more of these occur:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• A static discharge around the windshield</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• A bright glow in the engine inlets</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• Smoke or dust on the flight deck</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• An acrid odor</div>

<div class="reg-selector-bar">
  <button class="reg-btn" data-variant="ABPF">−ABPF to −ABQG</button>
  <button class="reg-btn" data-variant="ABPA">−ABPA to −ABPE</button>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Exit the volcanic ash as quickly as possible. Consider a 180 degree turn. Consider a descending turn.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Don the oxygen masks and smoke goggles, if needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Establish crew communications, if needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>Do <strong>not</strong> do the Dual Eng Fail/Stall checklist until directed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>If</strong> at any time during this checklist both engines fail:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Move both fuel control switches to CUTOFF, then RUN.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Push the ram air turbine switch and hold for 1 second.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">6</span>Do <strong>not</strong> do the ENG FAIL checklist until directed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>When</strong> in volcanic ash:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Minimize thrust if practical. This reduces EGT and engine damage.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">8</span><strong>ENGINE ANTI–ICE selectors (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">9</span><strong>APU selector (if APU available)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>START, then ON</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Volcanic ash can cause non–normal system reactions such as:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• Engine malfunctions, increasing EGT, engine stall, or flameout</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• Decrease or loss of airspeed indications</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• EQUIP CLG OVRD message</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• EQUIP COOLING FWD or AFT message</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• FIRE CARGO FWD or AFT message</div>
