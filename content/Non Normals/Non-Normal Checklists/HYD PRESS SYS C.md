<div class="nnc-deferred-title">Primary flap and slat operation inop</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">The slats and flaps are operating in secondary mode. Use the flap lever to operate the slats and flaps.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Allow 1.5 minutes to extend slats from UP to 1. Allow 2.5 minutes to extend flaps from 1 to 20.</div>

<div class="nnc-deferred-title">Landing gear normal extension inop</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Alternate gear extension is needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Nose wheel steering may be inoperative. The NOSE WHEEL STEERING message may show after gear extension.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> The slats will extend beyond midrange when airspeed is below 240 knots. For go-around, do not exceed 240 knots until the slats retract to midrange.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Use flaps 20 and v<sub>REF</sub> 20 for landing. This ensures adequate go-around performance due to slower flap and slat operation in secondary mode.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> The autothrottle will maintain a minimum speed of 5 knots above the amber band.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Roll rate may be reduced in flight. Speedbrake effectiveness may be reduced in flight and during landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not arm the speedbrake lever. This prevents inadvertent in-flight speedbrake extension.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Buffet can occur.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Manually extend the speedbrakes after landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> For go-around, do not exceed the gear EXTEND limit speed (270K / .82M).</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Do not move the flap lever after landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Check the Non-Normal Configuration Landing Distance tables in the Performance Inflight-QRH chapter or other approved source.</div>
