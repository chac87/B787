# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> NAV AIRSPEED DATA

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: An error is detected in the airspeed data.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Use the current airspeed indication on the PFDs and HUDs. The indication uses an accurate backup source (AOA SPD).</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Avoid abrupt control inputs and high maneuvering loads. Airplane response is changed by simplified control laws.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>A/T ARM switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>F/D switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Thrust asymmetry protection is inoperative. Maintain airspeed at or above 130 knots.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Airspeed is calculated using angle of attack. AOA SPD may be erratic during configuration changes, maneuvering, and speedbrake use.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>ANTI-ICE selectors (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON or OFF as needed</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>Tuning and control panel GPWS FLAP OVRD</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OVRD</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Envelope protection functions are inoperative.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not use the autopilot, autothrottle, or flight directors.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">The yaw damper is degraded.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Manual control inputs are needed to compensate for asymmetric thrust conditions.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Use flaps 20 and v<sub>REF</sub> 20 for landing. If v<sub>REF</sub> 20 is less than 130 knots, use 130 knots for v<sub>REF</sub> 20. This ensures sufficient pitch trim and directional control capability for landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Avoid speedbrake use in flight.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Do not arm the speedbrake lever. This prevents inadvertent in-flight speedbrake extension.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Manually extend the speedbrakes after landing.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Run the engine and wing anti-ice systems manually.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Some or all ground proximity alerts are not available. Ground proximity alerts that occur are valid.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Some or all windshear alerts are not available. Windshear alerts that occur are valid.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Check the Non-Normal Configuration Landing Distance tables in the Performance Inflight-QRH chapter or other approved source.</div>
