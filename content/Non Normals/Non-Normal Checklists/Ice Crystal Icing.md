# Ice Crystal Icing

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Engine ice crystal icing or TAT probe icing is suspected. Ice crystal icing conditions exist when in visible moisture, and one or more of the following indications are present:<br>• Amber or red weather radar returns below the airplane<br>• Appearance of liquid water on the windshield at temperatures too cold for rain (the sound is different than rain)<br>• Abnormal engine vibration indications without other abnormal engine indications <span style="font-style:italic;color:var(--gray)">(B787 -ABPA to -ABPE)</span> / The autothrottle is unable to maintain the selected airspeed <span style="font-style:italic;color:var(--gray)">(B787 -ABPF to -ABPU, -ABQA to -ABQG)</span><br>• TAT indication on EICAS stays near 0 degrees C<br>(Additional items that can indicate ice crystal icing are listed in the Additional Information section.)</div>

<div class="cl-variant">B787 (-ABPA to -ABPE)</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Minimize time above amber and red weather radar returns.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>If conditions allow, exit the ice crystal icing conditions laterally. Climbing or descending to exit ice crystal icing conditions is not recommended.</div>

<div class="nnc-complete">■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-deferred-header">Additional Information</div>

One or more of the following can indicate ice crystal icing:
- Light to moderate turbulence
- Static discharge around the windshield (St. Elmo's fire)
- Smell of sulfur
- Smell of ozone
- Humidity increase

Vibration indication due to fan hub icing can be above 4 units. Fan ice removal procedures have no effect on fan hub icing. When clear of clouds, fan hub ice sublimates and engine vibration decreases over time. Fan hub ice can remain into descent.

The ICA indication on EICAS can show in clear air during engine self-test or calibration.

<div class="cl-variant-end">END B787 (-ABPA to -ABPE)</div>

<div class="nnc-deferred-header">Additional Information</div>

One or more of the following can indicate ice crystal icing:
- Light to moderate turbulence
- Static discharge around the windshield (St. Elmo's fire)
- Smell of sulfur
- Smell of ozone
- Humidity increase

An erroneous TAT indication can occur as a result of ice crystals blocking the sensor. The erroneous indication can last from one minute to more than 20 minutes. TAT normally should increase approximately 2 degrees Celsius per 1000 ft of descent.
