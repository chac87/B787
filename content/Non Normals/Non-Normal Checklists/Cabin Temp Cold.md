# Cabin Temp Cold

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Flight deck or cabin temperature is excessively cold. The temperature may cause incapacitation.<br>Objective: To descend before the cabin or flight deck temperature causes incapacitation. If needed, to configure to provide alternate ventilation.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">1&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>above 34.000 ft:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 2</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is at or below 34.000 ft:</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 4</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Start a descent to 34.000 ft or lower.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>When</strong> at or below 34.000 ft:</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>AIR COND RESET switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push and hold for 1 second</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>Wait</strong> 10 minutes.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">6&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Cabin temperature is <strong>still excessively cold:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 7</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Cabin temperature is <strong>becoming warmer:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-separator"></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>FLT DECK TEMP control</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>C</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Set to full cold. This provides more even distribution of warm air to the aft passenger cabin.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">8</span><strong>CABIN TEMP control</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>C</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Set to full cold. This provides more even distribution of warm air to the aft passenger cabin.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span>Start a descent to an altitude where cabin temperatures are becoming warmer, or the lowest safe altitude, whichever is higher.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Under most conditions, cabin temperatures should become warmer above 22.000 ft. Under extreme conditions, a descent to as low as 10.000 ft may be necessary to increase cabin temperature.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">10</span>An airspeed increase will aid in cabin heating.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">11</span><strong>When</strong> at level off:</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">12</span><strong>Wait</strong> 10 minutes.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">13&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Cabin temperature is <strong>still excessively cold:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 14</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Cabin temperature is <strong>becoming warmer:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If a higher cruise altitude is needed due to range or other operational considerations, the crew may climb, then assess the cabin temperature at the new altitude.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">If temperature control is restored, the flight deck temperature control and cabin temperature control may be selected to a warmer temperature.</div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-separator"></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">14</span>Descend to the lowest safe altitude or 10.000 ft, whichever is higher.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">15</span>Plan to land at the nearest suitable airport.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">16&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>above 10.000 ft:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 17</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>at or below 10.000 ft:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 19</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">17</span>Don the oxygen masks.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">18</span>Establish crew communications.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">19</span><strong>PACK switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">20</span><strong>VENTILATION switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ALTN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">21</span>Selecting the VENTILATION switch to ALTN automatically reconfigures both outflow valves to optimal flow positions and can take up to 3 minutes to reduce cabin differential pressure.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">22</span><strong>Wait</strong> 3 minutes.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">23</span>The correct outflow valve positions are:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Forward outflow valve approximately 10 o'clock position</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Aft outflow valve 1 o'clock position</div>

<div class="nnc-step">24&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Both outflow valve positions are <strong>correct:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the alternate ventilation switch to NORM or ALTN as needed.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 29</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">One or both outflow valve positions are <strong>incorrect:</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 25</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">25</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-box">⚠&ensp;Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">26</span><strong>FWD OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move the forward outflow valve until the indication shows at the 10 o'clock position</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-box">⚠&ensp;Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">27</span><strong>AFT OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move to OPEN until the outflow valve indication shows at the 1 o'clock position</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the aft outflow valve between fully closed and the 1 o'clock position as needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">28</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklist:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• CABIN ALTITUDE AUTO</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">29</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklist:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• PACK L+R</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Bulk cargo heat is inoperative.</div>
