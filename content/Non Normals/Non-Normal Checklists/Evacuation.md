# Evacuation

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: An evacuation is needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">1</span><strong>PARKING BRAKE</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong><span class="nnc-role">C</span></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong><span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move to OPEN</strong><span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Move to OPEN until the outflow valve indications show at the 12 o'clock position to depressurize the airplane.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>FUEL CONTROL switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CUTOFF</strong><span class="nnc-role">C</span></div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span>Advise the cabin to evacuate.<span class="nnc-role">C</span></div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">6</span>Advise ATC.<span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>Engine fire switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Pull</strong><span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">8</span><strong>APU fire switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Override and pull</strong><span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span><strong>If</strong> an engine or APU fire is observed or indicated:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>Related fire switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Rotate to the stop and hold for 1 second</strong><span class="nnc-role">F/O</span></div>
