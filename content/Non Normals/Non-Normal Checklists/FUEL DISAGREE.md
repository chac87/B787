# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> FUEL DISAGREE

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The totalizer fuel quantity and the FMC calculated fuel quantity disagree.<br>Objective: To decide if a fuel leak is suspected. To select the most accurate fuel value if a fuel leak is not suspected.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>A fuel leak can be suspected if any of the following occur:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• Both engines are running and one has a higher fuel flow than the other.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• One main tank is abnormally low compared to the other main tank and to the expected fuel remaining in the tanks.</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• On CDU PROGRESS page 2, the TOTALIZER fuel is less than the CALCULATED fuel.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">2 &nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">A fuel leak is <strong>suspected</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ <a href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists/Fuel-Leak"><span>Go to the Fuel Leak checklist.</span></a></div>
</div>
<div class="nnc-complete">■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■ &nbsp; ■</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">No fuel leak is <strong>suspected</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 3</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Select PROGRESS page 2.</div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Select TOTALIZER unless it is inaccurate.</div>
