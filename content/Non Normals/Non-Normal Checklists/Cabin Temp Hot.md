# Cabin Temp Hot

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: Flight deck or cabin temperature is excessively hot. The temperature can cause incapacitation.<br>Objective: To descend and configure to provide alternate ventilation before the cabin or flight deck temperature causes incapacitation.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>The packs may not be controlling the temperature correctly, even if the switch OFF lights are extinguished.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>TRIM AIR switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>If</strong> airplane altitude is above 10,000 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">Start a descent to the lowest safe altitude or 10,000 feet, whichever is higher. Use the speedbrakes to increase the rate of descent, if needed.</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>IFE/PASS SEATS power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note">The IFE/passenger seats switch can be turned ON again when needed (such as moving powered seats for landing). Select the IFE/passenger seats switch off again when no longer needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>CABIN/UTILITY power switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>SHOULDER and FOOT HEATERS controls (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>LOW</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">7</span>Minimize flight deck lighting intensity.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">8</span>During daylight, install the flight deck sun visors.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">9</span>Plan to land at the nearest suitable airport.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">10</span><strong>When</strong> at level off:</div>

<div class="nnc-step">Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>above 10,000 feet</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 11</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>at or below 10,000 feet</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 13</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">11</span>Don the oxygen masks.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">12</span>Establish crew communications.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">13</span><strong>PACK switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">14</span><strong>VENTILATION switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ALTN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">15</span>Selecting the VENTILATION switch to ALTN automatically reconfigures both outflow valves to optimal flow positions and can take up to 3 minutes to reduce cabin differential pressure.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">16</span><strong>Wait</strong> 3 minutes.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">17</span>The correct outflow valve positions are:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">Forward outflow valve approximately 10 o'clock position</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">Aft outflow valve 1 o'clock position</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-step">18 &nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Both outflow valve positions are <strong>correct</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-note">If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the alternate ventilation switch to NORM or ALTN as needed.</div>
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 23</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">One or both outflow valve positions are <strong>incorrect</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 19</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">19</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">20</span><strong>FWD OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move the forward outflow valve until the indication shows at the 10 o'clock position</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-caution">Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switch to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">21</span><strong>AFT OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move to OPEN until the outflow valve indication shows at the 1 o'clock position</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-note">If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold or too warm, the crew can cycle the aft outflow valve between fully closed and the 1 o'clock position as needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">22</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklist:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">• CABIN ALTITUDE AUTO</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">23</span>If needed to reduce flight deck temperature, open the flight deck door.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">24</span>If needed to reduce flight deck temperature, open the flight deck overhead vent. Open the overhead door cover in the ceiling behind the first officer's seat. Then open the overhead vent cover panel for access to the flight deck overhead vent.</div>
<div class="nnc-note">Opening the overhead vent results in a very loud flight deck that can cause difficulty in communications. If unable to maintain communications due to noise, close the vent.<br><br>If the flight deck or cabin temperature becomes too cold, the crew can:<br>• Push the cabin/utility power switch ON<br>• Push the IFE/passenger seats power switch ON<br>• Select the shoulder and foot heaters controls above LOW<br>• Close the flight deck overhead vent<br>• Open or close the flight deck door<br>Bulk cargo heat is inoperative.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">25</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
<div class="nnc-sub">• PACK L+R</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• TRIM AIR L</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• TRIM AIR R</div>
</div>
