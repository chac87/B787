# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> GEAR DISAGREE

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The gear position disagrees with the landing gear lever position.<br>Objective: To extend the gear using alternate gear extension, or land on the available gear.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> <em>Do not exceed the gear EXTEND limit speed (270K/.82M).</em></div>

<div class="nnc-step">1&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing gear lever is <strong>UP</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> <em>Flight with the gear down increases fuel consumption and decreases climb performance. Refer to the Gear Down tables in the Performance Inflight chapter for flight planning.</em></div>
  <div class="nnc-text">Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">• AUTO SPEEDBRAKE</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">• GEAR DOOR</div>
  <div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Landing gear lever is <strong>DN</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 2</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>Speedbrake lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>DOWN</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>ALTN GEAR extend switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push to DOWN and hold until all gear indicate down or in transit</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">4</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• AUTO SPEEDBRAKE</div>
<div class="nnc-sub">• GEAR DOOR</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>Wait</strong> 30 seconds.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>Speedbrake lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> <em>Do not arm the speedbrake lever. This prevents inadvertent in-flight speedbrake extension. Manually extend the speedbrakes after landing.</em></div>

<div class="nnc-step">7&ensp;&ensp;Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision"><strong>All</strong> gear indicate <strong>DOWN</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-complete">■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■ &thinsp; ■</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision"><strong>Any</strong> gear indicate <strong>UP or in transit</strong>:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶&ensp;Go to step 8</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">8</span>Plan to land on the available gear.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">9</span><strong>Tuning and control panel GPWS GEAR OVRD</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OVRD</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> <em>Use flaps 30 for landing. This gives the slowest landing speed. Manual speedbrake extension gives better control of the touchdown. When stopping distance is critical, extend the speedbrakes after all gear, or the nose, or the engine nacelle have contacted the runway. Do not use the thrust reversers unless stopping distance is critical.</em></div>
