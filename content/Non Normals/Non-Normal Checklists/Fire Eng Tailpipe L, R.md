# Fire Eng Tailpipe L, R

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: An engine tailpipe fire occurs on the ground with no engine fire warning.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">1</span><strong>FUEL CONTROL switch (affected side)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CUTOFF</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">2</span><strong>Engine fire switch (affected side)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Verify In</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span>Advise the cabin.</div>
<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">4</span><strong>START selector (affected side)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>START</strong></div>
<div class="nnc-sub">This motors the engine if the APU or 2 external power sources are on.</div>
<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">5</span>Advise ATC.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>When</strong> the tailpipe fire is extinguished:</div>
<div class="nnc-sub-item"><strong>START selector (affected side)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>NORM</strong></div>
