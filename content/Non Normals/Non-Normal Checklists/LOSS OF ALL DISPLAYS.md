# LOSS OF ALL DISPLAYS

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: All five forward displays and both head-up displays are failed.<br>Objective: To restore partial display operation.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">1</span>Engage the autopilot only in <strong>HDG HOLD, TRK HOLD, HDG SEL,</strong> or <strong>TRK SEL</strong> roll mode; and <strong>V/S, FPA,</strong> or <strong>ALTITUDE HOLD</strong> pitch mode.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">2</span>Do <strong>not</strong> use LNAV, VNAV, FLCH, LOC/FAC, or APP modes.</div>
<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Autothrottle is inoperative.</div>

## Additional Information

> [!info]- True to Magnetic Heading Conversion
> To convert true heading to magnetic heading, do one of the following:
> - Subtract the eastern variation from true heading
> - Add the western variation to true heading
