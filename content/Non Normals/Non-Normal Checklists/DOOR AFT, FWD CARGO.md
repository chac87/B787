# <span class="h1-bracket">[]</span> DOOR AFT, FWD CARGO

<a class="nnc-back" href="/Non-Normals/Non-Normal-Checklists">Non-Normal Checklists</a>

<div class="nnc-condition">Condition: The cargo door is not closed and secure.<br>Objective: To reduce the cabin differential pressure to decrease the risk of door separation.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">1</span><strong>LDG ALT selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>PULL ON, set 8000</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-step">2&nbsp;&nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>at or below</strong> 8.000 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-sub">Level off at the lowest safe altitude.</div>
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 3</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>above</strong> 8.000 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-sub">Descend to the lowest safe altitude or 8.000 feet, whichever is higher.</div>
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 3</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">3</span><strong>After</strong> level off, allow sufficient time for the cabin altitude to stabilize. This minimizes discomfort when the airplane is depressurized.</div>

<div class="nnc-step">4&nbsp;&nbsp; Choose One:</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>at or below</strong> 10.000 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 5</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-decision">Airplane altitude is <strong>above</strong> 10.000 feet:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-sub">Don the oxygen masks.</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">Establish crew communications.</div>
  <div class="nnc-goto">▶▶ Go to step 5</div>
</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="nnc-num">5</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>MAN</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-box">Use momentary actuation of the outflow valve manual switches to avoid large and rapid pressurization changes.</div>

<div class="nnc-item"><span class="c-amber">⚠</span> <span class="nnc-num">6</span><strong>OUTFLOW VALVE MANUAL switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Move to OPEN until the outflow valve indications show at the 12 o'clock position to depressurize the airplane</strong></div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">7</span><strong>After</strong> the airplane is depressurized, the crew may change altitude as needed.</div>

<div class="nnc-text"><span class="nnc-num">8</span>Do <strong>not</strong> accomplish the following checklists:</div>
<div class="nnc-cl">
  <div class="nnc-sub">· CABIN ALTITUDE AUTO</div>
  <div class="nnc-sub">· LANDING ALTITUDE</div>
</div>
