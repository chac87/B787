# GPWS Response

### GPWS Caution
- Correct the flight path or the airplane configuration.
- Terrain caution under daylight VMC, and positive visual verification is made that no obstacle or terrain hazard exists: Alert may be regarded as cautionary and the approach may be continued.
- Time until impact: 40 – 60 sec

### GPWS Warning
- Do not use FD
- Time until impact: 20 – 30 sec

> [!proc]- GPWS Maneuver
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> „PULL UP – I have Control"
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> Disconnect AP, A/T
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> "Max Power"
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> Apply Maximum Thrust
> <span class="cm2">PM</span> Assure Maximum Thrust
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> Wings Level, 20° pitch
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> Verify Speedbrakes retracted
> <span class="cm2">PM</span> Assure All Actions completed
> <span class="cm2">PM</span> Monitor Altitude and V/S
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> If necessary: Pitch up to the pitch limit indicator or stick shaker or initial buffet
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> Do not change Config
> <span class="cm2">PM</span> Callout trend towards terrain
> <span class="cm1">PF</span> When clear of terrain: Decrease pitch and accelerate
