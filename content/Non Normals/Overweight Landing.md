# Overweight Landing

> [!info]- Overweight Landing
> Overweight landings may be safely accomplished using normal landing procedures and techniques. No adverse handling characteristics.
> - Landing distance is normally **less than T/O distance** for Flaps 20/25 at all gross weights
> - Verify wet/slippery runway field length from PI landing distance charts
> - Brake energy limits will **not** be exceeded
> - Accomplish **Overweight Landing NNC**
>
> If stopping distance is a concern: reduce landing weight as much as possible. At the captain's discretion consider fuel jettison, or reduce weight by holding at low altitude with a high drag configuration (gear down) to achieve maximum fuel burn-off.

> [!caution]- Overweight Autolands — NOT Recommended
> Autopilots are **not certified** for automatic landings above MLAW. Performance may not be satisfactory at higher than normal speeds and weights.
>
> If an automatic approach is attempted: disengage autopilot prior to flare height and accomplish a **manual landing**.
