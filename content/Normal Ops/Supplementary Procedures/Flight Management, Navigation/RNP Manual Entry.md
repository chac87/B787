# RNP Manual Entry

The FMC automatically supplies appropriate RNP values based on phase of flight. When the airplane is on a procedure or airway that has an RNP requirement, and does not have an RNP value stored in the navigation database, a manual RNP entry may be made.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>PROG key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>RNP PROGRESS page</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">RNP PROGRESS is the fourth page of PROGRESS.</div>

</div>

If the displayed RNP is different from the RNP for the current airway or procedure:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>RNP</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Enter</strong></div>

</div>

When the manually entered RNP is no longer required:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>PROG key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>RNP PROGRESS page</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">RNP PROGRESS is the fourth page of PROGRESS.</div>

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>RNP</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Delete</strong></div>

</div>
