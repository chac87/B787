# Navaid Inhibit

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>INIT REF key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>INDEX key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>POS</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>POS REF page</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">POS REF is the third page of POS INIT.</div>

</div>

To inhibit GPS updating:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-sub">GPS updating can be inhibited at pilot discretion if it is not needed or GPS data is unreliable. It is not recommended to disable GPS updating for the entire flight.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> GPS updating is needed for some mandatory navigation specifications.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Inhibiting GPS updating does not inhibit the GPWS look-ahead terrain function. The GPWS look-ahead terrain function can be inhibited by selecting GPWS TERR to OVRD on the TCP.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> Inhibit GPS updates for approach operations that are not based on WGS-84, unless other appropriate procedures are used.</div>

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>GPS NAV</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify GPS NAV OFF selected.</div>

<div class="nnc-note"><strong>Note:</strong> If GPS NAV is selected OFF, it is recommended that DME/DME, VOR DME, and LOC NAV updating be selected ON.</div>

</div>

To inhibit DME/DME updating:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>DME/DME</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify DME/DME OFF selected.</div>

</div>

To inhibit VOR/DME updating:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>VOR/DME</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify VOR/DME OFF selected.</div>

</div>

To inhibit localizer updating:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>LOC NAV</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify LOC NAV OFF selected.</div>

</div>

To inhibit data from a specific navaid:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>INIT REF key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>INDEX key</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-indent cl-item"><strong>NAV DATA</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-sub">Using scratchpad, enter identifiers of specific navaids or VORs to be inhibited on the NAVAID INHIBIT or VOR ONLY INHIBIT lines.</div>

</div>
