# Hot Weather Operations

During extended ground operations prior to flight deck preparation, consideration should be given to reducing the heat being generated on the flight deck. Window heat, radar, and other electronic components which contribute to a high temperature level on the flight deck may be turned off. All the flight deck air outlets should be open.

Both packs should be used for maximum cooling. Recirculation fans should be on for maximum cooling capacity. To maximize the cooling capacity of the air conditioning system, all doors, including cargo doors, should be kept closed as much as possible. All passenger cabin gasper outlets should be open. Passenger cabin windows should be fully dimmed. Flight deck cooling can be improved by closing the flight deck door and lowering the side trays adjacent to the pilot seats.

> [!warning] Caution
> Recirculation fans must remain ON unless a non-normal checklist or supplementary procedure directs to select them off.
