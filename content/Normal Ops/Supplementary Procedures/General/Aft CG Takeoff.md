# Aft CG Takeoff

Airplane operations with an aft CG configuration require additional considerations to accommodate the effects of reduced nose gear steering effectiveness. The procedures in this section supplement the normal procedures and should be observed when applicable.

The combination of aft CG, rapid thrust application, and sudden brake release can result in reduced directional control during the initial takeoff phase. Crosswinds and runway contamination can cause further reduction of directional control. If the value of CG (%MAC) is at or greater (more aft) than the value in the table below, the following procedures must be applied to ensure directional control during takeoff roll:

| 787-9 | 108 | 120 | 140 | 160 | 180 | 200 | 220 | 240 or greater |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **CG (%MAC)** | 23.1 | 24.7 | 27.4 | 29.8 | 32.1 | 34.4 | 36.8 | 37.5 |

- Use reduced thrust whenever possible.
- Release brakes before increasing engine thrust above idle.

> [!warning] Caution
> If a static engine run-up is needed before brake release, do not exceed 55% N1. Do not release the brakes with N1 greater than 55%. A tip-up can occur and directional control can be reduced.

<div class="cl-variant">B787 (-ABPF to -ABPU)</div>

Crosswind capability may be reduced if brakes are released above 40% N1. (Crosswind limits are determined by the operator. If the operator uses Boeing takeoff crosswind guidelines as limits, reduce crosswind limits by 5 knots when brakes are released above 40% N1.)

<div class="cl-variant-end">END B787 (-ABPF to -ABPU)</div>

<br>

<div class="cl-variant">B787 (-ABPU to -ABQU)</div>

Crosswind capability may be reduced if brakes are released above 20 TPR. (Crosswind limits are determined by the operator. If the operator uses Boeing takeoff crosswind guidelines as limits, reduce crosswind limits by 5 knots when brakes are released above 20 TPR.)

<div class="cl-variant-end">END B787 (-ABPU to -ABQU)</div>

- Maintain forward pressure on the control column until 80 knots to increase nose wheel steering effectiveness. Above 80 knots, relax the column pressure to the neutral position.
