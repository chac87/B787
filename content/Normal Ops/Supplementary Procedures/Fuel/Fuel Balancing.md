# Fuel Balancing

If a fuel leak is suspected, accomplish the Fuel Leak checklist.

The fuel balance system is inhibited if any of these occur:

- The fuel jettison system is active.
- The FUEL DISAGREE or FUEL QTY LOW message shows.
- On the ground and one or both engines are running.
- The fuel balance system is failed.
- A center tank pump is on.
- Refueling, defueling, or ground transfer of fuel is in progress.

## In Flight

If fuel balancing is necessary in flight and fuel balance operation is not inhibited:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL BALANCE switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push and hold for 1 second</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">The fuel balance system may take up to 30 seconds to activate.</div>

</div>

## On Ground

If fuel balancing is necessary while the airplane is on the ground and both engines are shutdown:

> [!info] Note
> The APU must be running.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>One fuel pump in the higher quantity tank</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL BALANCE switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push and hold for 1 second</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">The fuel balance system may take up to 30 seconds to activate.</div>

</div>

When fuel balance is complete:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL PUMPS AFT and FWD switches (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

</div>

## Fuel Balance Inhibited

If fuel balancing is necessary in flight or on the ground and fuel balance operation is inhibited with one or two engines running:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL CROSSFEED switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

</div>

If the left main tank quantity is low:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>L FUEL PUMPS AFT and FWD switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">This allows fuel from the higher quantity tank to feed both engines.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Do not accomplish the following checklists: FUEL PUMP L AFT · FUEL PUMP L FWD</div>

</div>

If the right main tank quantity is low:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>R FUEL PUMPS AFT and FWD switches (both)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">This allows fuel from the higher quantity tank to feed both engines.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Do not accomplish the following checklists: FUEL PUMP R AFT · FUEL PUMP R FWD</div>

</div>

When fuel balancing is complete:

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL PUMPS AFT and FWD switches (all)</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL CROSSFEED switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

</div>
