# CDU/EFB Preflight Procedure – PF


<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Initial Data</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>SET</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>CDU IDENT page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the MODEL is correct</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify the ENGINES are acc. OFP</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the ENG RATING is correct</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the navigation database ACTIVE date range is current</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the DRAG/FF parameter is consistent with the OFP</div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>CDU POS INIT page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the time is correct</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Enter the present position on the SET INERTIAL POS line, if needed. Use the most accurate latitude and longitude</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Navigation Data</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>SET</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>CDU RTE page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Enter the route</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Enter the FLT NO.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">ACTIVATE and execute the route</div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>CDU DEPARTURES page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Select the runway and departure routing</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Execute the runway and departure routing</div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>POS REF page 2</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify the correct RNP for the departure, as needed</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the route is correct on the RTE page. Check the LEGS pages as needed to ensure compliance with the flight plan</div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>NAV RADIO page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Tune the navigation radios as needed</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Performance Data</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>SET</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>CDU PERF INIT page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Enter the ZFW. Use OFP-Value</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the FUEL on the CDU, the OFP and EICAS agree</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the fuel is sufficient for flight</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the GR WT on the CDU and the OFP agree</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Perform preliminary T/O Data calculation</div>
<div class="cl-sub"><strong>THRUST LIM page</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the OAT is displayed</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Required Briefings</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>PERFORM</strong></div>

</div>

> [!info] Note
> If an EOSID contained in the NAV-DB is used, refer to SP 2-2-11 Usage of EOSID contained in FMS-Database in FMS.

> [!info] Note (B787 -ABPA to -ABPE)
> If gross weight CG (%MAC) is at or aft of 23.1, refer to Aft CG Takeoff.
