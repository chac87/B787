# Before Taxi Procedure
<div class="checklist">

<span class="sop-tag-items">C: "BEFORE TAXI Items – FLAPS ___"</span>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that all engine generator OFF lights are extinguished. — F/O</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>APU selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF — F/O</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>ENGINE ANTI-ICE selectors</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed — F/O</strong></div>

<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the ground personnel and equipment are clear. — C, F/O</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Flap lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set takeoff flaps — F/O</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Flight controls</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Check — C</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Make slow and deliberate inputs, one direction at a time.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Move the control wheel and the control column to full travel in both directions and verify:</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· Freedom of movement</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· That the controls return to center</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Hold the rudder pedal disconnect switch down during the rudder check to prevent nose wheel movement.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Move the rudder pedals to full travel in both directions and verify:</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· Freedom of movement</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· That the rudder pedals return to center</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Transponder</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>XPDR — F/O</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Secondary Engine Indications</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off — F/O</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Recall</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Check — C, F/O</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that only expected alert and memo messages are shown.</div>

</div>
