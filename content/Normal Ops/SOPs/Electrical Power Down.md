# Electrical Power Down

The following procedure is accomplished to remove all electrical power from the airplane.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>IRS selectors</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>BATTERY switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FD DOOR POWER switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>EMER LIGHTS switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>PACK switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>EFB POWER switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>

</div>

Verify HYD PRESS SYS L+C+R message is displayed.

> [!caution] Caution
> The APU or external power must remain ON for the specified cool-down period following pack and hydraulic EMP shutdown. The cool-down period allows the Power Electronics Cooling System (PECS) to remain powered, dissipating residual heat from the liquid-cooled LRUs. With the APU providing power, or with both forward external power receptacles connected and switches selected ON, wait **10 minutes** before removing power. With one forward external power receptacle connected and switch selected ON, wait **22 minutes** before removing external power.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>APU selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>EXTERNAL POWER switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>External power sources</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Removed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that all external power AVAIL lights are extinguished.</div>

</div>
