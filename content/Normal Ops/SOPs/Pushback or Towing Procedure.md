# Pushback or Towing Procedure

The Engine Start procedure may be done during pushback or towing.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Ground handling personnel</strong> <span class="nnc-role">C</span><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Establish communications</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">For GND Communication refer to GND-COC Communication.</div>
<div class="cl-caution">Do not turn the nose wheel tiller during pushback or towing. This can damage the nose gear or the tow bar.</div>
<div class="cl-caution">Do not use airplane brakes to stop the airplane during pushback or towing. This can damage the nose gear or the tow bar.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Transponder</strong> <span class="nnc-role">F/O</span><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>XPDR</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Parking brake</strong> <span class="nnc-role">C</span><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set or release</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Set or release as directed by ground handling personnel.</div>

<div class="cl-sub">When pushback or towing is complete:</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>Tow bar</strong> <span class="nnc-role">C</span><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Verify not connected</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>Nose gear steering</strong> <span class="nnc-role">C</span><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Verify not locked out</strong></div>

</div>
