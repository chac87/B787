# Preliminary Preflight Procedure – First Officer

<div class="cl-note">The Preliminary Preflight Procedure assumes that the Electrical Power Up supplementary procedure is complete.</div>
<div class="cl-note">Refer to PeOM Aircraft EFB Startup (Pilots eOPS Manual).</div>
<div class="cl-note">If the CREW OXYGEN LOW alert message is shown, the flight crew oxygen pressure dropped below 1.200 PSI. Check if oxygen pressure is sufficient for the upcoming flight. Refer to 5-3-1-2 Flight Crew Oxygen Dispatch Pressure Required (column 2 Cylinders).</div>

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Security Check</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>PERFORM</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>IRS selectors</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF 30 seconds, then ON</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the ON BAT light is extinguished</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>STATUS display</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CHECK</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that only expected messages are shown</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the following are sufficient for flight: Oxygen pressure, Hydraulic quantity, Engine oil quantity</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>EICAS display</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CHECK</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that only expected alert and memo messages are shown</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Do the remaining actions after a crew change or maintenance action</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify the CREW OXYGEN LOW alert message is blank</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Maintenance documents</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CHECK</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Emergency equipment</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CHECK</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">For Quantity and Location refer to Notausrüstung (FSM)</div>
<div class="cl-sub">For functional Check refer to General Part Notausrüstung (FSM)</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Flight deck overhead door</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Closed and latched</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Emergency Entry Request</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>TEST</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>CB</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CHECK</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Push the systems (SYS) display switch on the display select panel, then select the CB key. Verify no CBs are open. Otherwise call maintenance</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>BROADBAND COMM switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Parking brake</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Set the parking brake if the brake wear indicators are to be checked during the exterior inspection</div>

</div>
