# Preflight Procedure – Captain
> [!info]- Preflight Flow
> ![[preflight flow.webp]]

The captain normally does this procedure. The first officer may do this procedure if needed.

<div class="checklist">

<div class="cl-item"><strong>EFIS control panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>MINIMUMS reference selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>RADIO or BARO</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>MINIMUMS selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set decision height or altitude reference</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>FLIGHT PATH VECTOR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>METERS switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>BAROMETRIC reference selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>IN or HPA</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>BAROMETRIC selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set local altimeter setting</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND mode selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select MAP</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND mode select switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>MENU</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">Map selections — As needed</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND CENTER switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND range selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>0.5, 1, 2 or 5</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">Displays airport map.</div>
<div class="cl-caution cl-indent">Do not use the Airport Map application as a primary navigation reference. The Airport Map application is designed to aid flight crew positional awareness only.</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND WXR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">Verify that the weather radar indications are not shown on the ND or PFD mini-map.</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ND TFC and TERR switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Display select panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>EICAS TRANSFER switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">Select EICAS to the desired side.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Mode control panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>FLIGHT DIRECTOR switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ON</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>AUTOTHROTTLE ARM switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>ARM</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>Autopilot DISENGAGE bar</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>UP</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>HEADING/TRACK reference switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>BANK LIMIT selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>AUTO</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>VERTICAL SPEED/ FLIGHT PATH ANGLE reference switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ALTITUDE increment selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Oxygen</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Test and set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>Oxygen mask</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Stowed and doors closed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>RESET/TEST switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Push and hold</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">Verify that the yellow cross shows momentarily in the flow indicator.</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>RESET/TEST switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Release</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>Emergency/100%/Normal selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>100%</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FORWARD PANEL brightness control</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Mid position</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>INBOARD and OUTBOARD DISPLAY brightness and CONTRAST controls</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Mid position</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>AIR DATA/ATTITUDE source selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>AUTO</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>PFD/MFD selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>NORM</strong></div>
<div class="cl-note">Do the Initial Data and Navigation Data steps from the CDU/EFB Preflight Procedure and verify that the IRS alignment is complete before checking the flight instruments.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Flight instruments</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Check</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the flight instrument indications are correct.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that only these flags are shown:</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· TCAS OFF</div>
<div class="cl-variant">B787 (-ABPA to -ABPE)</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· TFC OFF</div>
<div class="cl-variant-end">END B787 (-ABPA to -ABPE)</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· NO VSPD until takeoff V-speeds are selected</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the flight mode annunciations are correct:</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· Autothrottle mode is blank</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· Roll mode is TO/GA or HUD TO/GA</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· Pitch mode is TO/GA</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">· AFDS status is FLT DIR</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Select the MAP mode.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Integrated standby flight display</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the approach mode display is blank.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Set local altimeter setting.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the flight instrument indications are correct.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that no flags or messages are shown.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>SPEEDBRAKE lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>DOWN</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Reverse thrust levers</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Down</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Forward thrust levers</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Down</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Flap lever</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">The flap position indicator does not show when the flaps are up.</div>
<div class="cl-sub">Set the flap lever to agree with the flap position.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Parking brake</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Verify that the PARKING BRAKE SET message is shown.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>STABILIZER cutout switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Guards closed</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL CONTROL switches</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>CUTOFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>FUEL CONTROL switch fire warning lights</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Verify extinguished</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>ALTERNATE FLAPS panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ALTERNATE FLAPS ARM switch</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Off</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>ALTERNATE FLAPS selector</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>OFF</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Left tuning and control panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Set</strong></div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>WEATHER RADAR</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">GAIN and MODE — As needed</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>TRANSPONDER</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">CAPT TCAS Display — As needed</div>
<div class="cl-sub cl-indent">TCAS ALT — As needed</div>
<div class="cl-item cl-indent"><strong>VHF</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Select</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Captain's audio control panel</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>As needed</strong></div>
<div class="cl-warning">Do not put objects between the seat and the aisle stand. Injury can occur when the seat is adjusted.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Seat</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Adjust</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Adjust the seat for optimum eye reference.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Rudder pedals</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Adjust</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Adjust the rudder pedals to allow full rudder pedal and brake pedal movement. Stow the rudder pedal adjust crank.</div>
<div class="cl-caution">Turn the rudder pedal adjust crank no faster than approximately one turn per second to avoid damage. Do not apply force to the pedals during adjustment.</div>
<div class="cl-warning">If the rudder pedal adjust crank is not stowed after use, the rudder pedals may move out of the desired position.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Fuel Quantity</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Check</strong></div>
<div class="cl-sub">Check quantity and distribution.</div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>Seat belt and shoulder harness</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Adjust</strong></div>

<div class="cl-item"><strong>HUD Seal</strong><span class="cl-dots"></span><strong>Check for Damage</strong></div>
<div class="cl-note">If HUD seal is found broken or damaged make a TLB entry. (Item is certified as cabin minor).</div>

</div>

## PREFLIGHT

| Item | Response | Who |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| **Gear Pins and Covers** | Removed | PM |
| **Oxygen** | Tested, 100 | All |
| **Flight instruments** | Heading\_\_, Altimeter\_\_, \_\_ | C, F/O |
| **Parking brake** | Set | C |
| **FUEL CONTROL switches** | CUTOFF | C |
| **Fuel quantity** | \_\_tons | C, F/O |

The CM who has performed the exterior inspection will answer for Gear Pins and Covers.

C reads QTY from EICAS, F/O announces fuel ordered.
